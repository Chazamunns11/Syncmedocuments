# Tendril — Brand Naming Research & Shortlist

> **Decision: the name is Tendril** (chosen from the shortlist below), pending the live
> domain/trademark verification in §6. Backups: Grove, Lodestar.

Synthesis of multi-source deep research (2025–2026) on naming a coach-first CRM.
**Critical caveat:** live domain availability and trademark status **cannot** be verified by
web research — every candidate below MUST be checked manually (see §6 checklist) before adoption.
Several competitor/registrar figures came from secondary sources (vendor pages blocked fetch).

---

## 1. What the research says (naming principles)
- **Go suggestive/brandable, not descriptive.** Descriptive names ("CoachCRM") give instant clarity
  and keywords but are the **hardest to trademark** (generic terms aren't protectable) and weak in the
  AI-search era. Distinctive names trademark cleanly and get cited by answer engines. *Middle path:*
  a name that *hints* at the category without naming it (Salesforce, not "Sales Software Co.").
- **Short + sayable.** ≤2 (ideally) or 3 syllables; pass the **radio test** — a stranger should both
  *pronounce* and *spell* it on hearing it. No numbers, hyphens, or cute misspellings.
- **TLD: .com preferred, not required.** Google ranks all TLDs equally; .co/.io/.app/.coach are
  credible, especially for a tech-leaning audience. Prioritize getting your **exact** name over forcing
  a .com — but .com still signals credibility to non-technical buyers (coaches) and avoids sending
  traffic to whoever owns the .com.
- **Don't box yourself in.** Avoid prefixing with **"Coach___"** or any single-function word — the #1
  cause of expensive rebrands (DroneBase→Zeitview, Dunkin' Donuts→Dunkin'). Name the *feeling/benefit*,
  not the niche, so you can expand later.
- **Owning the domain ≠ trademark rights.** A prior trademark holder in your class (SaaS = US Class 42 /
  UK equivalent) can force a rebrand regardless of who owns the domain. Check both.
- **Meaning is often retrofitted.** Kajabi (learned the meaning *after* buying the domain) and Dubsado
  (a toddler's nonsense word) prove the **ownable, brandable name comes first**; the story comes later.

## 2. The two camps (and which we want)
- **Soft/evocative real or coined words** → *premium, simple, brand-forward* positioning.
  HoneyBook, Bonsai, Satori, Dubsado, Kajabi, Notion, Stripe. **← This is Tendril's lane.**
- **Descriptive compounds** → *feature-stuffed, does-everything* positioning.
  CoachAccountable, GoHighLevel, ClickFunnels, ActiveCampaign. (The bloat brands we're positioned against.)

## 3. Names to AVOID (already major brands / saturated)
From the research, these have high collision or saturation risk in/near our space:
**Cadence** (Salesloft Cadence + Cadence Design Systems — strong CRM-space collision),
**Beacon** ($250M-funded co), **Compass** (real-estate giant), **Circle** (Circle.so),
**Bloom / Sprout** (Sprout Social), **Pulse / Loop / Flow** (saturated), **Thrive** (Thrive Global),
**Ally** (bank), **Mentor** (Siemens), **Unity** (game engine).
Also avoid saturated affixes: `-ly`, `-ify`, `-hub`, `-flow`, `-ai`, `Smart-`.

## 4. Why "Tendril" over plain "Tend"
- **Pro:** semantically perfect ("runs your follow-ups for you" = tending your client garden), short,
  warm, 1 syllable, passes the radio test.
- **Con:** there is a **well-known dental/wellness brand "Tend"** (owns `tend.com`). Different class
  (dental services, not SaaS) so likely *legally* usable, but it muddies search/SEO and the clean .com
  is gone — you'd need `tend.coach`, `gettend.com`, `usetend.com`, or `tendapp.com`.
- **Verdict:** keep as a contender, but the domain/SEO friction means a more ownable alternative may win.

## 6. Finalize-the-name checklist (do before committing)
Run this on your top 2–3 (you're UK-based — check **both** UK and US):
1. **Domain (exact-match):** bulk-check via Instant Domain Search or Namecheap "Beast Mode"
   (free, up to 5,000 at once). Prefer `.com`; acceptable fallbacks `.coach`, `.co`, `.app`.
2. **Social handles:** Namechk / Namecheckup across X, Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, YouTube, GitHub.
3. **UK trademark:** search the **UK IPO** database for identical/similar marks in the SaaS class
   (file fee ~£200 +£50/extra class — *verify*, fees rose ~April 2026; "Right Start" service de-risks).
4. **US trademark:** search the **USPTO** Trademark Search (Class 42 = SaaS) for "likelihood of
   confusion" (~$350/class to file).
5. **EU (if selling in EU):** EUIPO search — separate registry from UK post-Brexit.
6. **Foreign-meaning check:** make sure it isn't rude/odd in major languages.
7. **Radio test:** say it to 5 people; can they spell it back?
8. **File intent-to-use** once decided — secures priority before launch.

## 7. Decision
**Chosen: Tendril** — keeps the warm "tend" root, adds the "reaching out to follow up" metaphor, and is
far more ownable than plain "Tend" (whose `.com` is held by a dental brand). Backups if the §6 checks
block it: **Grove**, then **Lodestar**.
